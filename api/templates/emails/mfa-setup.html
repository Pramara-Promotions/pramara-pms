🔒 What is MFA?
Multi-Factor Authentication requires both your password and a time-based code from your authenticator app to log in. This ensures that only you can access your account, even if someone knows your password.

Setup Instructions:

  1. Open your authenticator app (Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator, Authy, etc.)
  2. Scan the QR code displayed in your account settings
  3. Enter the 6-digit code from your app to verify
  4. Save your backup codes in a secure location
